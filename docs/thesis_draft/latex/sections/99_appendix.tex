\paragraph{Instrument verification discipline.} Three consecutive, opposite-signed flaws were
found in the gates of our own schema diagnostic (an extractor that over-counted failures, a
shape gate that over-counted successes, and a content gate that under-counted the untrained
baseline), each caught by opening raw outputs rather than trusting the aggregate rate. The
project therefore adopted a standing rule: any rate-type, gate-decided number must have ten
deterministically-sampled raw decisions (passes and failures) inspected by a human before it
enters a table or figure. A suspiciously clean number is treated as an instrument fault until
its raw outputs have been read.
