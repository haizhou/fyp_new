01 — The corpus becomes a graph从原始记录到图谱

166,277 releases, and none of them are ready to query

166,277 条公告,没有一条能直接拿来查询

886,324nodes节点
2,040,212edges边
77platform identities for the Ministry of Defence, in one year国防部在单一年度内出现的平台身份数

Three problems make the raw data unusable directly. One organisation appears under many platform account identifiers and name variants. Signed values often live in contract sub-records rather than the nominal award-value field. And values reported at tender, lot, award and contract level do not mean the same thing, so some can be added together and some are the same money counted twice.

原始数据有三个问题,任何一个都足以让直接查询失效。

一、身份碎裂。同一个机构会以多个平台账号识别码和名称变体出现。
二、金额藏在别处。真正签约的金额经常在合约子记录里,而不是名义上的授标金额字段。
三、金额不可比。招标、标段、授标、合约四个层级报出来的金额意义不同,有些能相加,有些相加就是同一笔钱算两次。

Every one of those is a correctness problem rather than an engineering inconvenience. A query can be syntactically perfect, execute without error, and still return the wrong number because it summed a framework ceiling once for every call-off beneath it.

关键在于:这三件都是正确性问题,不是工程上的不便。一个查询可以语法完美、执行无误,却依然返回错误的数字——因为它把框架协议的上限金额,在底下每一笔订购中都重复加了一次。

Two thirds of the edges are provenance links from evidence back to the record it supports. That is deliberate. They are what lets an answer carry its own audit trail.

三分之二的边是溯源链接,把证据指回它所支撑的记录。这是刻意设计的:正是这些边让每个答案自带审计轨迹。

Entity resolution is precision-first

实体消解以精确度优先

Merging runs as a waterfall, and each tier only fires when the tier above cannot decide.

合并采用瀑布式:只有上一层无法判定时,下一层才启动。

  • Registry identifiers merge first, because they are externally verifiable.
  • Safe deterministic variants next, where the transformation is reversible.
  • Normalised name plus regional evidence after that.
  • Fuzzy similarity never writes a merge. It only nominates candidates for review.
  • 注册机构识别码最先合并,因为可以对外验证。
  • 安全的确定性变体其次,条件是该转换可逆。
  • 规范化名称加地区证据再次之。
  • 模糊相似度永远不直接写入合并,只能提名候选交人工复核。

204,711 raw aliases resolve to 131,502 organisations, and 82,013 singletons stay unmerged rather than be forced into a cluster. Over-merging two councils is a silent, permanent error in every answer downstream. Leaving them apart is a visible one.

204,711 个原始别名收敛成 131,502 个机构,其中 82,013 个单例宁可不合并,也不强行分群。

理由:错误合并两个议会,会在下游每一个答案里造成无声且永久的错误;让它们分开,错误至少看得见。

02 — The benchmark评测基准

12,828 questions, each one born from a program that already ran

12,828 道题目,每一道都诞生自一支已经跑过的程序

The benchmark is generated program-first. A parameterised program is instantiated and executed against the graph, and only then is a natural-language surface written for it. Every row therefore ships with an executable gold program and a computed answer, rather than an answer someone believed was correct.

这个基准是程序优先生成的:先把参数化程序实例化、在图谱上执行,之后才为它撰写自然语言题面。

结果是每一行都自带一支可执行的黄金程序和一个计算出来的答案,而不是某个人认为正确的答案。

Composition

组成

9,267 training rows, three development slices, and a sealed test of 2,285. Splits separate program structures, not surface wording, so a paraphrase cannot leak across the boundary.

9,267 条训练数据、三个开发切片、2,285 题密封测试集。切分依据是程序结构而非表面措辞,所以改写过的同义句不会跨边界泄漏。

Refusal is in the label space

拒答是标签空间的一部分

Ambiguous, unsupported, and empty-result questions are first-class rows with their own gold status, not errors. A system is scored on recognising them.

语义含糊、数据模型不支持、查无结果这三类题,都是有自己黄金状态的一等公民,不是错误。系统会因为能否识别它们而被计分。

Gates before admission

入库前的闸门

Every program is re-executed against the full graph before its question is admitted, rejecting multi-answer ambiguity, plan overlap, duplicate identifiers and local sampling omissions.

每支程序在题目入库前都会对完整图谱重新执行一次,借此排除:多重答案歧义、计划重叠、识别码重复、局部采样遗漏。

A benchmark I generated myself is not evidence on its own. So a second evaluator, written in pure pandas and sharing no execution code with the system, recomputes every oracle from the raw tables.
自己生成的基准,本身不构成证据。所以我写了第二个求值器:纯 pandas 实现,与系统不共用任何执行代码,从原始数据表重新计算每一个标准答案。

That audit is also where the real bugs surfaced. The first pass agreed on 92.7%, and crucially the disagreements clustered by question family rather than scattering, which made them diagnosable. Three genuine defects fell out: bridge oracles assumed a flat first-supplier universe that had never been written into the gold; an empty-string supplier name was silently matching every record without a supplier, injecting a constant £1.61 billion; and 230 constraints were degenerate. After those fixes, agreement is 99.88%, or 14,752 of 14,770 cases. The remaining 18 were each inspected by hand and none affects a reported comparison.

真正的错误也是在这场审计中浮现的。第一轮一致率只有 92.7%。

关键线索:不一致的案例是按题型成簇出现,而不是随机散落——这让它们变得可诊断。三个真实缺陷因此被揪出来:

一、桥接类标准答案隐含假设了「展平后取第一供应商」的宇宙,但这个约定从未写进黄金数据。
二、一个空字符串的供应商名称,悄悄匹配了所有没有供应商的记录,注入固定 16.1 亿英镑误差。
三、另有 230 个约束是退化的。

修正后一致率 99.88%(14,770 例中的 14,752 例)。剩下 18 例逐一人工看过,没有一例影响已报告的比较。

Runnable on this laptop, right now

现在就能在这台机器上跑

.venv/bin/python scripts/interview_demo.py --only audit

Recomputes 40 sealed test rows from the raw node and edge tables in about 19 seconds. No model, no GPU, no network.

从原始节点与边数据表重新计算 40 条密封测试题,约 19 秒。不需要模型、GPU 或网络。

03 — The architecture系统架构

The model writes the program. Execution writes the answer.

模型负责写程序,执行负责写答案

language model语言模型 deterministic code确定性代码
stage 1Reader理解器

Question in, structured briefing out: answer type, explicit atoms, the intermediate sets the answer depends on, direction of each relational role, ambiguity flags. No data access.

输入问题,输出结构化简报:答案类型、显式原子、答案依赖的中间集合、每个关系角色的方向、歧义标注。这一步不接触数据。

stage 2Planner规划器

Question and briefing in, typed program out. Selects an intent template from a closed inventory, fills typed slots, emits strict JSON under a grammar compiled from the algebra.

输入问题与简报,输出类型化程序。从封闭的意图模板集合中选型,填入类型化槽位,并在由算子代数编译出的语法约束下产出严格 JSON。

stage 3Schema grounding字段接地

Field text to canonical slot through candidate generation, a type gate, a confidence threshold and a top-2 margin. Relation phrasing beats role nouns, so “awarded to X” makes X the supplier.

把字段文本映射到规范槽位,经过候选生成、类型闸门、置信阈值、前二名差距检查。关系措辞优先于角色名词,所以「awarded to X」判定 X 是供应商。

stage 4Entity grounding实体接地

The same threshold-and-margin discipline against the organisation registry. Never takes the top hit blindly. Variant equivalence classes and abbreviation expansion.

对机构登记表套用同样的阈值与差距纪律,绝不盲目采用第一名命中,并处理变体等价类与缩写展开。

stage 5Compile编译

Twelve normalisation transforms turn recognisable planner idioms into executable structure, then three gates: whole-program groundability, DAG validity, no unconstrained bind sources.

十二个规范化变换,把可识别的规划器惯用写法转成可执行结构。随后三道闸门:全程序可接地、DAG 合法性、无未受约束的绑定源。

stage 6Execute执行

Dependency-levelled evaluation with bind propagation, exact decimal arithmetic, contract-level deduplication, the additivity guard and date-aware comparison. Returns the answer and the records behind it.

按依赖层级求值,含绑定传播、精确十进制运算、合约级去重、可加性防护、日期感知比较。返回答案以及支撑它的记录。

stage 7Verify验证

Uniqueness where one record is claimed, additivity where money is summed, population coverage where a set is enumerated. Incomparable comparison sides raise an error rather than quietly returning False.

声称单一记录时查唯一性,汇总金额时查可加性,枚举集合时查总体覆盖。比较双方不可比时直接抛错,而不是悄悄返回 False。

stage 8Repair, gated修复(带闸门)

Fires only on a diagnosable defect. Refusals and meaningful empty results are final. An earlier ungated version turned six correct refusals into confident wrong answers.

只在出现可诊断的缺陷时才启动。拒答与有意义的空结果是终局。早期没有闸门的版本,把六个正确的拒答「修」成了自信的错误答案。

Why a closed algebra rather than SQL

为什么用封闭代数而不是 SQL

Seven value types and seventeen operators. Narrow, deliberately, because narrowness buys three properties that matter more here than expressiveness.

七种值类型、十七个算子。刻意做窄,因为窄换来三个在这个场景比表达力更重要的性质。

  • Membership is decidable by a recursive type checker, so an invalid program is rejected with a typed diagnostic and a path into the tree.
  • The grammar compiles to a JSON schema, so constrained decoding makes malformed output impossible rather than unlikely.
  • It is small enough to reimplement independently, which is the only reason the 99.88% audit was possible at all.
  • 可判定。是否属于这个语言由递归类型检查器判断,非法程序会带着类型诊断和树中路径被拒绝。
  • 可编译成 JSON schema。因此受约束解码让格式错误成为不可能,而不只是不太可能。
  • 小到可以被独立重新实现。这正是那场 99.88% 审计得以成立的唯一原因。

The design constraint behind all of it

贯穿整个设计的那个约束

A program can compile, ground and execute perfectly and still compute the wrong quantity.
一支程序可以完美地编译、接地、执行,却依然算出错误的量。

That single fact drives two decisions. Training acceptance is oracle match, not execution success, because a program that runs cleanly proves nothing about whether it answered the question asked. And verification is a separate stage from execution, checking properties of the result that the executor has no reason to doubt.

这一件事推导出两个决策:

一、训练的采纳标准是对上标准答案,而不是执行成功——顺利跑完完全无法证明它回答的是被问的那个问题。
二、验证是独立于执行的阶段,专门检查那些执行器没有理由去怀疑的结果性质。

The student passes the teacher that trained it

学生超过了训练它的老师

85.65strongest fully local system, sealed test最强全本地系统,密封测试
69.76the cloud teacher that generated its training data生成其训练数据的云端老师

The honest reading has four parts, and I would rather give them than have them extracted.

诚实的解读有四点。我宁可主动讲出来,也不要等别人来挖。

It is a system comparison, not a capability claim

这是系统比较,不是能力宣称

The student is trained in-domain; the teacher is evaluated zero-shot as a deployment. This shows the local system is more accurate on this task. It does not show an 8B model is generally stronger than a frontier one.

学生接受了领域内训练,老师是以零样本部署的形式被评测。

所以它说明的是:本地系统在这个任务上更准确。它不说明 8B 模型的整体能力超过前沿模型。

The filter, not the teacher, carries the signal

带来信号的是过滤器,不是老师

Candidates are kept only if they compile, ground, execute and reproduce the oracle, so the student learns from a distribution cleaner than the teacher's own output. Rejection sampling on the student's own verified traces, with the teacher entirely absent, held level with supervised training. That is the cleanest evidence for this reading.

候选程序必须通过编译、接地、执行并重现标准答案才会被保留,所以学生学到的分布比老师自己的输出分布更干净。

最硬的证据:用学生自己验证过的轨迹做拒绝采样、老师完全缺席,成绩与监督式训练持平。说明带来信号的是过滤器,不是持续访问老师。

Decoding is not equal, and I report it that way

解码条件并不对等,我如实报告

Every local system decodes under the same compiled grammar; the teacher API cannot. On the challenge set, 23.1% of the teacher's rows fail on format alone. So this is a comparison of deployable systems, not of models under identical conditions.

所有本地系统都在同一套编译语法下解码,老师的 API 做不到。在挑战集上,老师有 23.1% 的题目仅仅因为格式就失败。

所以这是可部署系统之间的比较,不是同等条件下的模型比较。

It replicates on a second base

在第二个基座上可复现

The same ordering holds with Llama-3.1-8B as with Qwen3-8B, which makes it less likely to be an artefact of one model family. Fully local also beats hybrid on both bases, by 7.61 points on the stronger one.

换成 Llama-3.1-8B,排序与 Qwen3-8B 一致,降低了「这只是某个模型家族的假象」的可能性。全本地在两个基座上都胜过混合式,在较强的基座上领先 7.61 分。

The three limits I would fix next

我接下来会修的三个极限

  • Universal quantification. Disclosure and compliance questions sit at 38.8%, the weakest family for every system including the teacher. They need a claim about every notice of a buyer, and my algebra expresses that badly.
  • Genuinely new logical shapes. Unseen intent shapes cost 11.5 points. New compositions of familiar constructs transfer well; new forms do not.
  • Single training runs. Every trained system is one run, so I have test-set significance but no training-seed variance. That is the weakest methodological point in the project and I would fix it first.
  • 全称量化。信息披露与合规类题目停在 38.8%,是所有系统(包括老师)最弱的一族。它们需要对某买方的每一则公告做出断言,而我的代数表达这件事的能力很差。
  • 真正没见过的逻辑形状。未见过的意图形状要付出 11.5 分代价。熟悉构件的新组合迁移得很好,全新的形式则不然。
  • 单次训练。每个训练系统只跑了一次,所以我有测试集上的显著性,却没有训练种子的方差。这是整个项目方法论上最弱的一点,也是我会最先修的。

What actually limits the transfer

真正限制迁移的是什么

Rather than guess, I ran a differential audit against 11,276 human-written SQL programs for the same questions and measured which ones my algebra can express.

与其猜测,我对同一批题目的 11,276 支人工撰写 SQL 程序做了差分审计,直接测量我的代数能表达其中哪些。

Coverage rose from 53.9% to 61.0% after compiler and loader closure, and 39.6% remains outside the language. Retraining after that intervention improved both supervision regimes, which is the clearest evidence that the gap is representation, not planning.

编译器与加载器闭包之后,覆盖率从 53.9% 升到 61.0%,仍有 39.6% 落在语言之外。

关键验证:这项干预之后重新训练,两种监督形式同时提升——这是「差距来自表示而非规划」最清楚的证据。

I would rather report that number than hide it. It is the honest price of choosing a closed algebra, and knowing it precisely is what tells me where to spend the next month.

我宁可把这个数字报出来,也不愿藏起来。它是选择封闭代数所付出的诚实代价——而正因为知道得够精确,我才知道下个月该把力气花在哪里。

08 — Where this meets your work这与你们的产品在哪里交汇

Bishop reads structure. That is the problem I have been living in.

Bishop 读的是结构,而我这一年就住在这个问题里

What I would bring to Bishop

我能为 Bishop 带来什么

  • A measured case for why the product needs to exist. A strong model over retrieved rows gets 3.7% on exhaustive numeric questions, and 3.3% even when handed a perfect plan. That is not a tuning problem.
  • A typed interface that makes a number reviewable. Every answer arrives with the program and the records behind it, which is the precondition for a finance or procurement team acting on it.
  • A recipe for running it locally. An 8B model above a frontier cloud model on the task matters most when the customer's data cannot leave their building.
  • 一份量测过的、关于这个产品为何必须存在的论据。强模型读着检索回来的记录,在穷举型数值问题上只有 3.7%;即使把完美的计划直接交给它,也只有 3.3%。这不是调参能解决的问题。
  • 一个让数字可被复核的类型化接口。每个答案都带着产生它的程序与记录一起送达——这正是财务或采购团队愿意据以行动的前提。
  • 一套让它在本地跑起来的做法。当客户的数据不能离开他们自己的机房时,「8B 模型在这个任务上胜过前沿云端模型」这件事最有价值。

What I would bring to Donnie

我能为 Donnie 带来什么

An organisational memory that confidently reports a decision nobody took is worse than no memory at all. That failure mode is the one I have measured most carefully.

一个会自信地报告出「根本没人做过的决定」的组织记忆,比没有记忆更糟。而这正是我量测得最仔细的失效模式。

  • Abstention as a scored capability, not an afterthought. 138 of 138 unsupported questions recognised while still answering 83% of the answerable ones.
  • Provenance attached to every claim, so what the organisation decided is checkable rather than trusted.
  • A negative result worth knowing. My planner lost abstention against an older system because its training mix carried only 37 refusal demonstrations. Competence follows demonstrations, including the competence to say “I don't know”.
  • 把弃答当成一项计分能力,而不是事后补丁。138 题不支持的问题全部识别出来,同时仍然答对 83% 的可答题。
  • 每一个主张都附带溯源,让「组织到底决定了什么」变成可核查的,而不是只能相信。
  • 一个值得知道的负面结果。我的规划器在弃答上输给了一个更旧的系统,原因是训练配方里只有 37 个拒答示范。能力跟着示范走,包括「说我不知道」这项能力。
The questions that need both products at once are the interesting ones. “What did we agree with this supplier, and what have we actually spent with them?” is a words query and a numbers query joined on an entity.
真正有意思的,是那些同时需要两个产品的问题。「我们和这家供应商谈定了什么,以及我们实际上花了多少钱?」——这是一个文字查询和一个数字查询,在同一个实体上做连接。

That handoff is exactly the seam my architecture is built on. A reader does the language understanding and hands a typed, checkable specification to an engine that computes. If Donnie and Bishop are one platform, the contract between them is the interesting engineering problem, and it is the one I have already had to design once.

这个交接处,正是我的架构所建立的那道接缝:理解器负责语言理解,然后把一份类型化、可检查的规格交给计算引擎。

如果 Donnie 和 Bishop 是同一个平台,它们之间的契约就是最有意思的工程问题——而这个问题我已经被迫设计过一次了。
